# ADR Template

## Title

A short, descriptive title for the decision.

## Status

Proposed / Accepted / Deprecated

## Context

Explain the problem that requires a decision. Include relevant constraints, goals, and background.

## Decision

Describe the choice that was made.

## Alternatives Considered

List the alternatives that were evaluated and why they were rejected.

## Consequences

Describe the expected impact, both positive and negative.

## Related Documents

Link to any relevant rules, workflows, or design notes.
